# Evidence Atlas

The composition treats the application as evidence. Full frames establish context, while explicitly labelled component renders reveal text that would otherwise require scrolling. Nothing in a captured interface is retouched, repaired, or replaced.

A quiet paper background, dark text and thin dividers distinguish the editorial surface from the application. Numbering carries navigation order; short captions state the screen, appearance and capture dimensions. Meticulous alignment and consistent spacing serve comparison.

Scale follows the source pixels. Screens retain their proportions, and original screenshots are supplied separately. The canvas grows to accommodate readable evidence rather than compressing the application into decorative thumbnails. Careful presentation must preserve awkward states as faithfully as successful ones.

The two sheets move from broad context to selected detail. The first follows storage discovery into cleanup review. The second follows capacity and history into explanations and evidence. Limitations are printed on both sheets so the quality of presentation never implies native interaction verification.
